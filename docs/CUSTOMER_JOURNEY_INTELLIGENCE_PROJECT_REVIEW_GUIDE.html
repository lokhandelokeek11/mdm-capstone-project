1. Executive Summary & Core Value Proposition

In modern e-commerce, businesses capture millions of digital touchpoints (clicks, product views, category browsing, cart additions, search queries). However, over 85% of visitors remain in browsing/inactive stages, and raw transaction rates often fall below 0.1%. Traditional marketing automation relies on blunt, batch campaigns (broadcasting generic discount codes or blast emails) that:

  • Trigger Marketing Fatigue & Unsubscribes: Annoying casual browsers with unsolicited emails.
  • Cannibalize Margin: Giving unnecessary discounts to high-intent shoppers who were already going to buy.
  • Lack Timing Relevance: Failing to understand where a customer is in their dynamic journey timeline.

JourneyIQ solves this by acting as an AI Decision Brain: It continuously models event sequences, quantifies purchase propensity & churn risk, segments shoppers into dynamic behavioral cohorts, and prescribes the Next Best Marketing Action (NBMA)—including explicit, non-intrusive WAIT & SUPPRESS decisions that preserve brand equity and marketing spend.

2. End-to-End System Architecture

The platform is organized into five decoupled layers operating across Python ML pipelines, a Next.js API server, and a React Vite frontend:

Layer 1 & 2: Data Ingestion & Journey Processing

  • Ingestion: Cleans raw events.csv, category hierarchy (1,669 nodes), and item stock status (36.8k catalog properties).
  • Sessionization: Groups sequential interactions using an industry-standard 30-minute inactivity boundary.
  • Journey State Engine: Maps dynamic customer progress across 7 stages: Inactive → Initial Engagement → Exploration → Consideration → High Intent → Conversion → Retention.

Layer 3 & 4: Predictive AI & NBMA Decision Engine

  • Sequence Modeling: GRU Deep Neural Network predicting the exact next touchpoint (View, Cart, Purchase).
  • Propensity Engine: Journey-aware XGBoost & Logistic Regression evaluated on PR-AUC.
  • NBMA Decision Matrix: Multi-criteria policy engine evaluating purchase probability, journey stage, churn risk, and catalog stock to select actions (Email, Discount, Cart Reminder, Wait, Suppress).

3. Research Methodology & Experimental Protocol (Paper Sec. IX-A)

No Future Peeking (Strict Chronological Cutoff): In e-commerce forecasting, random train/test splits cause severe data leakage (predicting past events using future observations). We enforce a strict chronological cutoff at τ = 80th percentile of dataset timestamps.

1. Training Set (70%)

24,500 visitors with all behavioral features extracted exclusively before τ.

2. Validation Set (10%)

3,500 visitors used for tuning XGBoost scale_pos_weight = 2.0 on validation PR-AUC.

3. Held-Out Test Set (20%)

7,000 visitors evaluated on post-τ ground truth (0.07% true conversion rate).

Understanding the Metrics (PR-AUC vs ROC-AUC)

ROC-AUC can be deceptively high (~0.99) with 99.9% true negatives. PR-AUC (Precision-Recall AUC) focuses strictly on positive convertors. Static Logistic Regression achieved the highest held-out test PR-AUC (0.1483).

7. Examiner Viva & Review Questions Cheat Sheet

Q1: How did you prevent data leakage during model training?

Answer: We implemented a strict chronological split using a cutoff timestamp τ (80th percentile). All customer behavioral features were calculated strictly from events occurring at or before τ, while target labels (purchase vs non-purchase) were derived exclusively from events occurring after τ. We avoided random splits which leak future interactions into the training set.

Q2: Why did you use PR-AUC instead of standard Accuracy or ROC-AUC for RQ3?

Answer: Our dataset exhibits severe class imbalance (0.07% post-τ purchase rate). In such distributions, a baseline model that predicts "no purchase" for 100% of users achieves 99.93% accuracy but has zero utility. ROC-AUC is distorted by large true-negative counts. PR-AUC directly evaluates the precision-recall trade-off on rare positive buyers.

Q3: Why did the Generic strategy win on Prectarget in RQ4, and why is Journey Intelligence still superior?

Answer: In offline holdout simulation, generic broadcast touches 100% of visitors, catching all rare buyers by brute force. However, it sends 7,000 spam messages. Journey Intelligence intentionally trades brute-force coverage for 4,391 non-intrusive WAIT/SUPPRESS decisions (62.7%), reducing unnecessary marketing interruptions by 47.7% and protecting customer lifetime value.

Q4: What is the role of SHAP in your Next Best Action decision engine?

Answer: SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) provides transparent feature attribution. For each prediction, SHAP calculates the exact marginal contribution of signals like stage_code, total_events, and recency_days, allowing marketing analysts to understand why an action was recommended rather than trusting a black box.
